Privacy Policy

This policy explains how account, portfolio, and session data are processed for platform operations.

Data Usage

Registration, login sessions, investments, and withdrawals are processed and retained for account servicing, audit trails, and support operations.

Security

Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2 with per-user salts, and access controls are enforced on protected account and admin endpoints.

Transparent Fees

Withdrawal fees are disclosed at request time and deducted automatically; no hidden release fees are enforced.
